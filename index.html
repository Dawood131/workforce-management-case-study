Overview

What it is

A workforce management system used inside a company's internal operations. Employees check in and out, request leave, and view payroll details, while admins monitor attendance and manage the workforce from one operations dashboard. The product runs in a live production environment.

Ownership

My role

I worked as a frontend developer in a cross-functional team, building frontend modules and integrating them with REST APIs. The core backend logic was handled by the backend team, and I contributed basic-level API work alongside them where frontend and backend responsibilities overlapped.

Breakdown

What's built into it

The product covers four areas of workforce operations.

01

Operations Dashboard

Summarizes workforce activity with live counts and trend charts, along with notifications for important events.

  • Headcount, presence, and leave metrics
  • Attendance trend charts
02

Attendance & Leave

Employees check in and out in real time, with location-based validation, and submit leave requests that go through an approval flow.

  • Check-in/check-out with live status
  • Leave requests and approvals
03

Payroll

Salary details are presented in a clear breakdown view, driven by attendance and leave records.

  • Salary breakdown and payslip view
04

Administration

An admin layer for managing accounts, company settings, and content shown to staff.

  • Account and company settings
  • Content management
