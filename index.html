Overview

What it is

A workforce management system used inside a company's internal operations. Employees check in and out, request leave, and view payroll details, while admins monitor attendance, track company stock, and manage the workforce from one operations dashboard. The product runs in a live production environment.

My role

Breakdown

What's built into it

The product covers four areas of workforce operations.

01

Core

An operations dashboard with live counts and trend charts, plus notifications for important events.

  • Headcount, presence, and leave metrics
  • Notifications
02

Workforce

Employees check in and out in real time with location-based validation, and submit leave requests that go through approval.

  • Attendance with live status
  • Employee management
  • Leave requests
03

Operations

Day-to-day company operations, from salary to stock to office locations.

  • Payroll with salary breakdown and payslip view
  • Inventory to track company stock
  • Locations to add and verify work sites
  • Messaging
04

Administration

Access control and company setup, so each person sees and does only what their role allows.

  • Roles with permissions per feature
  • Multi-level approvals across different roles
  • Members and security settings
  • Company profile, features, and news
